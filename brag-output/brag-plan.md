# Brag Plan: TripStash

## What is this app?
TripStash is a travel PWA that reads a backpacker's plan back as a map: numbered stops joined by a dashed line, nights as the stored truth and dates derived from them, every city carrying the traveller's own sentence, and an assistant that only ever answers with a source.

## The angle
"A backpacker's plan, read back as a map." The video is the plan becoming a route in front of you: first the whole trip on one map, then the stops with their nights, then the cities with the traveller's own Hebrew sentences, then what other travellers said about one stretch (four named sources), then the trip check catching a missed Carnival and fixing it with one tap. Nothing invented: every screen is the running app with the demo trip.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Two lines on the night ground, one after the other: "A backpacker's plan," / "read back as a map." A phone rises into frame at 2.65s showing the real Trip screen: twenty numbered pins down Central America to Brazil, the dashed route between them.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The itinerary: numbered stops with their dates and the nights steppers, panning slowly like a plan being read.
- Three city cards sliding in one by one: the plan's own photo, the traveller's Hebrew sentence (right to left), the nights badge, the places count.
- "What travellers say about this stretch": four source groups arriving one by one, Gringo (Hebrew), Reddit, YouTube, the web, each snippet with its link.
- The trip check: "קרנבל ריו דה ז'ניירו: Feb 5–Feb 10 … Stay 9 nights in Rio de Janeiro", the fix button highlighted.

## Outro / punchline
TripStash. "Works offline. No paid API." and, smaller, "Found clips are never counted as yours."

## User flow worth showing
Open the trip (the route draws itself) → press into a country (city cards with your sentence) → press + between two stops (what travellers say about that stretch) → read the trip check and press "Stay 9 nights in Rio de Janeiro".
